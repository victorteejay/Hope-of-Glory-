About Us

Who We Are

Hope Of Glory Schools is a reputable educational institution situated in Araromi, Barracks Area, Saki, Oyo State. We provide quality education from creche level through to upper basic, ensuring a strong foundation for every child.

Our school was founded with a vision to raise champions — children who are academically excellent, morally upright and socially responsible.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, nurturing and academically stimulating environment where every child is encouraged to discover their potential and develop into a well-rounded individual.

Our Vision

To be the leading educational institution in Saki and across Oyo State, producing confident, creative and compassionate leaders for the next generation.

Our Values

  • Excellence in academics and character
  • Respect for every child's unique ability
  • Integrity in all we do
  • Partnership with parents and the community
  • Faith-based foundation

Our Staff

We have qualified, experienced and passionate teachers and support staff dedicated to the growth and success of every student in our care.
