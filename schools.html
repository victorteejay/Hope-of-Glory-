Our Schools

Hope Of Glory Schools offers a complete educational journey from the earliest years of childhood through to upper basic level. Each school is designed to meet the developmental needs of children at every stage.

🍼 Creche

Our creche provides a safe, loving and stimulating environment for our youngest learners. We focus on early childhood development through play, exploration and care.

  • Ages 3 months to 2 years
  • Qualified childcare staff
  • Safe and hygienic environment
  • Structured play activities
  • Nutritious meals provided

🌱 Lower Basic

The lower basic school lays the academic foundation for young learners. We introduce children to literacy, numeracy and creative thinking in a fun and engaging way.

  • Ages 3 to 5 years
  • Literacy and numeracy foundation
  • Arts and crafts
  • Music and physical education
  • Social and emotional development

📚 Basic

Our basic school builds on the foundation laid in lower basic, introducing a broader curriculum that prepares pupils for the upper basic level and beyond.

  • Ages 6 to 10 years
  • English Language and Mathematics
  • Basic Science and Technology
  • Social Studies and Civic Education
  • Yoruba Language
  • Preparation for Common Entrance

🎓 Upper Basic

The upper basic school prepares students for senior secondary education and the challenges beyond. We focus on academic excellence, character development and leadership skills.

  • Ages 10 to 15 years
  • English, Mathematics and Sciences
  • Social Sciences and Humanities
  • Computer Studies
  • Preparation for and BECE
